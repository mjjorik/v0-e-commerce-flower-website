# Product — Wildflower

> Purpose of this file: durable product and brand context for people and agents working on Wildflower. It is not displayed on the website.

## Register

brand

## Users

People in Greater Boston choosing fresh flowers and small gifts for delivery, often for a specific occasion and often with limited time. They need to understand the offering quickly, trust the studio, and select a bouquet or occasion without friction.

## Product Purpose

Wildflower is a florist brand and WooCommerce storefront for hand-tied bouquets, subscriptions, delivery, and thoughtful add-ons across Greater Boston. Success means a visitor feels the work is real and considered, finds an appropriate occasion or bouquet quickly, and can order with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Studio-made, generous, quietly confident. The site should feel like a florist's working studio with real flowers and real delivery moments, not a generic online flower marketplace.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic florist templates, stock-photo collage layouts, decorative animation with no purpose, and dense ecommerce card grids that make the studio feel anonymous. Do not confuse Wildflower with Boston Flowers or reuse its business data.

## Design Principles

1. Let real flowers, delivery moments, and the people behind the work carry the page.
2. Give each section one clear job and enough breathing room to do it.
3. Motion should help a visitor discover choices, never delay or obscure them.
4. Preserve a warm studio character while keeping ordering paths immediate and legible.
5. Keep production media optimized so visual richness does not compromise page speed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard-access requirements. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; all information and navigation must remain available without autoplay or scroll-driven motion. Decorative images use empty rendered alt text when adjacent text already supplies their meaning; descriptive gallery and content images retain meaningful Media Library alt text.
